Platform safe zones

Platform UI sits on top of the image. Keep the product and any text out of these zones — the striped bands below are approximate reserved areas, not exact platform specs (they change; check before a major campaign).

Print

Print formats

For lookbooks, press kits and packaging inserts — margins are more generous than digital since there's no platform crop to worry about, only the trim.

A4 / Letter

20mm margins minimum, 3mm bleed if edge-to-edge colour is used. Collection World documents and press kits.

Business card

85×55mm, 3mm safe margin from trim on every side. Logo centered, nothing closer than the clear-space rule.

Packaging insert

Match the box's die-line exactly — never scale a digital layout down and hope it fits.
