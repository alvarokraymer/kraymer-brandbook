KNY

Every blade remembers the hand that carried it before. A world of inherited duty, quiet grief and discipline passed down like a family crest.

The completed Collection World document for this collection — the reference every asset, campaign and prompt should be checked against.

The 7 fields

Definition

01

Core Idea

Every blade remembers the hand that carried it before.

02

Mood

Formal, elegiac, seasonal. Restraint that carries real weight underneath.

03

References

Family crests and haori patterning, checkered and floral textile motifs, lacquerware, forest-at-dusk light.

04

Symbols

Checkerboard fragment, single-blade silhouette, a floral crest reduced to one repeated petal, cord-wrapped hilt detail.

05

Colour

Near-black base, deep oxblood accent, muted moss green secondary, warm ivory highlight.

06

Creative Applications

Photography with warm, low, directional light — late-afternoon interior. CGI backdrops textured like aged textile, never flat colour. Copy carries formality, slightly literary.
