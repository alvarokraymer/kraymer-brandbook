World 01 — Live

JJK

Power has a cost, and the ledger is always visible. A world built on curses, contracts and the discipline required to hold something dangerous without being consumed by it.

The completed Collection World document for this collection — the reference every asset, campaign and prompt should be checked against.

The 7 fields

Definition

01

Core Idea

Power has a cost, and the ledger is always visible.

02

Mood

Tense, disciplined, nocturnal. Dark academia with a current of dread underneath the composure.

03

References

Monastic uniforms and school-issue tailoring, ink-black calligraphy, exorcism talismans, fluorescent-lit corridors at night.

04

Symbols

Sealed-eye motif, binding cord, fractured circle, a single accent stroke against black.

05

Colour

Near-black base, bone-white contrast, one sickly violet accent — used only in accent pieces, never as a base colour.

06

Creative Applications

Photography lit hard from a single source with shadow retained, not lifted. CGI on black backdrops, minimal reflection. Copy short, clipped, declarative.
