GI

Every element has a temperature, a sound and a material — never a costume. Elemental identity expressed through material and finish, not through obvious signalling.

The completed Collection World document for this collection — the reference every asset, campaign and prompt should be checked against.

The 7 fields

Definition

01

Core Idea

Every element has a temperature, a sound and a material — never a costume.

02

Mood

Open, bright, elemental. Fantasy without whimsy — closer to natural science than storybook.

03

References

Mineral cross-sections, weather systems, regional textile dyeing techniques, architectural glasswork.

04

Symbols

An elemental mark reduced to a geometric glyph, wind-line etching, a single gemstone keyed to the element's colour.

05

Colour

Warm ivory base; one elemental accent per collection, rotating release to release — never blended within a single piece.

06

Creative Applications

Photography clean, bright, near-shadowless. CGI environments abstracted to colour and atmosphere, never a literal location. Copy plain and descriptive — the material carries the fantasy.

07

Do's & Don'ts

What belongs in this world

Do
Don't
Commit to one element per collection
Combine more than one elemental palette in a single piece
Let finish signal the element — matte for earth, high-polish for water
Rely on colour alone to communicate the element
Keep the palette limited to base + one accent
Use rainbow or multi-element gradients — reads as generic fantasy
