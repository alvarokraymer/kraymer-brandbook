AI Studio

The image can change.
The product cannot.

A shared production and quality-control process for AI-assisted product imagery — the full e-commerce catalogue, collection heroes, campaign imagery, advertising assets, social content and product-led motion.

The objective is not simply to produce visually convincing images. Every final asset must represent the original jewellery accurately, follow the approved visual direction and remain consistent with the wider brand system. AI output is production material requiring comparison, correction and approval — never a final decision on its own.

Prompting

Prompting system

The references define the product. The prompt defines how it is presented. A production prompt should be structured, measurable and easy to revise.

Be specific

"Seven round-cut red stones" is more useful than "several red gemstones."

Avoid contradictions

Do not request an extremely shallow depth of field while requiring the entire piece to remain sharp.

Correct one variable at a time

Geometry, materials, lighting, background and composition should be refined separately.

Repeat critical constraints

Repetition is useful when it protects the product, but useless if not.

Don't solve everything in one generation

Lock the jewellery first. Build the scene around it afterwards.

AI is a great prompter

Use any LLM to generate the prompt. Make sure you say what model you are generating on.

Quality

      
Error severity

      
Not every issue has the same impact. This system distinguishes between errors that misrepresent the product, issues that must be corrected before approval and minor imperfections that do not affect accuracy.

      

        

          
Critical
Reject or rebuild
Errors that misrepresent the product.

          
    
            
  • Wrong product or variant
  • Altered silhouette or proportions
  • Missing or added components
  • Incorrect gemstone count
  • Wrong metal
  • Incorrect chain or clasp
  • Incorrect engraving
  • False scale
  • Major geometry failure
    • 
          

        

        

          
Major
Correct before approval
Errors that compromise physical credibility or catalogue consistency.

          
    
            
  • Incorrect background
  • Inaccurate roughness or finish
  • Wrong gemstone colour or setting
  • Broken or fused links
  • Floating product
  • Incorrect contact
  • Contradictory lighting
  • Unnatural reflections
  • Visible AI artefact
  • Incorrect model interaction
    • 
          

        

        

          
Minor
Correct where practical
Small cosmetic issues that do not alter the product.

          
    
            
  • Slight background variation
  • Small edge halo
  • Minor reflection inconsistency
  • Local sharpness mismatch
  • Isolated artefact outside the jewellery
