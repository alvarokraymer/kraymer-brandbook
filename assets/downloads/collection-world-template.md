# Collection World · Template

Fill this out before any photography, CGI, video, campaign design or social production begins. Store it in the collection's main Drive folder.

## 01 · Core Idea
One sentence that defines the central concept of the collection.

## 02 · Mood
The emotional atmosphere the collection should communicate.

## 03 · References
Visual, cultural and artistic references that inform the direction.

## 04 · Symbols
Recurring elements, details or motifs connected to the collection.

## 05 · Colour
The primary palette and supporting accents.

## 06 · Creative Applications
How the world should translate into photography, CGI, video, web and social content.

## 07 · Do's & Don'ts
What belongs within the world, and what moves away from it.
